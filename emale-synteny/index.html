READING THE PLOT

A map of conserved genome structure

Arrow length encodes gene length; arrowheads show transcription direction. Pale ribbons connect aligned regions in neighboring genomes. Teal end blocks mark terminal inverted repeats; broad translucent bands mark Ngaro retrotransposons. The gray wiggle tracks show GC fraction in 50 bp windows.

SOURCE & REPRODUCIBILITY
